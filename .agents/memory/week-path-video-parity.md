---
name: Week path video parity
description: Keep the public landing clip aligned with the editable animation artifact.
---

The landing page plays a fixed MP4 while the editable week-path animation is a separate video artifact. Changes to its scenes or music do not update the landing clip automatically; refresh the MP4 and poster together when the animation changes.

**Why:** The public page needs a native, click-to-play video with a poster and browser playback controls, whereas the video artifact auto-plays its composition as an editable preview. Embedding that preview directly would not meet the visitor playback requirements.

**How to apply:** For future week-path video edits, review the landing copy and regenerate the delivered clip/poster from the updated animation before publishing.
