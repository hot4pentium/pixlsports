---
name: Tracker result versus score
description: Product decision for manual outcomes and unrecorded Stat Tracker scores.
---

A supporter can explicitly mark a win, loss, or tie even if they did not enter a final score. Treat the marked outcome as intentional, even when it differs from the entered score. Without an explicit choice, infer the result only from a recorded score, never from an unentered 0–0 scoreboard.

**Why:** Families may track plays without recording the scoreboard, or correct the result separately. A default zero scoreboard should not silently become a final tie.

**How to apply:** In history, reports, completion feedback, and shared season records, prefer the explicit result and label an unentered score as not recorded. Older games with an actual score can still derive the result from that score.

The native dashboard game record uses the selected same-sport tracker history, including when the HYPE Card is private or public stats are switched off. Do not merge independent saved histories or count practices and Live Taps-only sessions. Label it a game record rather than implying calendar-season filtering that the selected history does not enforce.

**Why:** The user requested a dashboard win/loss/tie graphic; account owners need their actual record independently of what they choose to publish. Independent saved histories may contain duplicate games.

**How to apply:** Keep private dashboard totals separate from public visibility controls, retain the established PWA result rules, and refresh the totals with the schedule after confirmed game changes without adding a per-tap record request.
