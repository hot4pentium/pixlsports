---
name: Dashboard and Showcase navigation
description: Dashboard HYPE Card previews open the private Player Showcase owner page.
---

The dashboard HYPE Card image is a clickable owner entry on mobile and desktop. It opens the private Player Showcase, where the owner can review the interactive card and find its editing, athlete-details, and sharing controls. Keep public sharing separate from private owner navigation, and preserve the Showcase return link to Supporter Hub.

**Why:** The user now wants the dashboard card to follow the iPhone interaction: tap the card to open its owner content page.

**How to apply:** Make the artwork a labeled, keyboard-accessible link to the owner Player Showcase, not to the public card. Keep public links in their explicit share actions and never use the dashboard card tap to bypass account ownership checks.

When the owner opens the public HYPE Card from Player Showcase, its back button must return to the private Player Showcase, not the separate public Showcase card view. Public recipients retain a public return destination.

**Why:** The user reported being sent to a different HYPE Card view instead of returning to the Showcase page.

**How to apply:** Preserve the owner entry context while ownership loads, and distinguish verified owners from ordinary recipients when resolving the card's back button.

The desktop card-design editor should follow the mobile sequence: card with its four content buttons, frame and lower-third arrows, then photo-position sliders.

**Why:** The user requested the same mobile-style design layout on desktop rather than a side-by-side card and large template gallery.

**How to apply:** Keep the single-column editor bounded and centered, and use the same arrow controls on both desktop and mobile.

Keep the desktop Showcase and its downstream management and public-card pages compact, centered, and consistently bounded. This includes the Edit HYPE Card entry buttons and both owner and athlete editing routes, not just public pages. Avoid oversized previews, headings, and excessive panel whitespace; preserve phone layouts.

**Why:** The user said the Showcase and its subsequent pages were too large and overwhelming on desktop, then repeated that the Edit HYPE Card entry and editing pages also needed better sizing and formatting.

**How to apply:** Apply density adjustments across the owner Showcase, manager, public Showcase, and standalone card together. Preserve card proportions, readable controls, and the established navigation.
