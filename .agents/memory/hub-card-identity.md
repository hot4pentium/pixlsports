---
name: Hub card identity
description: Identity safety for the Supporter Hub's saved HYPE Card preview and destination.
---

Do not treat having only one saved HYPE Card as evidence that it belongs to the current athlete. When saved identity does not match the selected athlete and team, offer setup/manage rather than showing or linking another athlete's card.

**Why:** Pairing the mobile schedule with saved artwork makes the older sole-card fallback visibly misleading after an athlete or team change. The request explicitly forbids showing another athlete's or account's card.

**How to apply:** Keep the preview and its full-card destination based on the same identity decision. Account-scoped loading or failures must not substitute another user's cached artwork.
