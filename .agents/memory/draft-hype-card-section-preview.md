---
name: Draft HYPE Card section previews
description: Keep onboarding section previews local and distinct from published card content.
---

Onboarding previews of Replies, Stats, Taps, and Events must use clearly labeled example content and local selection state. Do not mount the public/saved-card experience or trigger save/share requests while previewing.

**Why:** New users need to inspect card sections before they have saved or shared a card; the preview must not require publication or expose real supporter data.

**How to apply:** Keep draft preview controls in the onboarding editor, label all invented details as examples, and treat section selection as presentation-only state.
