---
name: Athlete identity visuals
description: Keeps supporter account identity separate from the athlete represented in game-day tools.
---

Only show a photo beside a named athlete when the image comes from an athlete-specific profile or media source. A signed-in supporter’s account photo must not stand in for the athlete; use the athlete’s initials when no athlete-owned image is available.

**Why:** Pixl Sports is supporter-first, so the signed-in account often belongs to a parent or fan rather than the athlete being tracked. Reusing that account image can visibly misidentify the athlete.

**How to apply:** Any athlete avatar, stat target, public profile, or report should verify the image source belongs to the athlete. Otherwise render a neutral initials fallback with the athlete’s accessible name.
