description: User-corrected boundary for the signed-out Celebrate demo's card behavior.

The signed-out Celebrate demo should show a photo-front Hype Card with three choices: Card front, Stats, and Events. Do not replace those choices with a generic greeting or message side.

**Why:** The user rejected a front/message flip as unlike the actual Hype Card, then explicitly removed video from the card and landing page.

**How to apply:** Keep the three controls functional within the card and demonstrate populated stats and events with clearly fictional content. Avoid video copy, thumbnails, account-backed data, or saving/sharing from the demo.
