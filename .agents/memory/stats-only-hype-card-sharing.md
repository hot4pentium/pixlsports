---
name: Stats-only Hype Card sharing
description: Local video sharing uses a hosted finalized-stats link without uploading the exported media.
---

Stats-only Hype Card shares keep the image/video on the creator’s device and publish only privacy-safe finalized athlete stats. Hosted player-page shares remain available as a separate mode.

**Why:** The product promise is that Pixl Sports is not involved in transporting the video when families share through their normal messaging or social apps.

**How to apply:** Keep media claims and object-storage paths optional only for the stats mode; require a linked tracker and finalized-stat projection for that mode, and preserve the existing hosted-card contract for card shares.
