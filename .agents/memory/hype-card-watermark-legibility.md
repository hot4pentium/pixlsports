---
name: Hype Card watermark legibility
description: Visual check for translucent branding on small card previews.
---

Treat the phone-sized editor preview as a primary readability target for photo overlays, not just the full-resolution export.

**Why:** An initially small, low-opacity watermark passed geometry and raster checks but looked too faint when the card was displayed around 320 pixels wide. The live preview revealed the problem that markup tests missed.

**How to apply:** When adjusting overlay typography or contrast, inspect the portrait card at phone width as well as the full-resolution output. Check light and dark media, keep branding clipped to the photo area, and preserve enough transparency to leave the athlete visible.
