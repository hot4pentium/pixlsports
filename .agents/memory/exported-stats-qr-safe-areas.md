---
name: Exported stats QR safe areas
description: Durable constraints for putting a scannable finalized-stats destination into downloaded Hype Card media.
---

The finalized-stats QR treatment must be rendered by the shared SVG foreground so still exports and every video frame receive the same destination, contrast, quiet zone, and fallback text.

**Why:** Native phone playback controls and existing card copy occupy the lower edge differently across portrait, square, card, and landscape outputs. A single bottom-edge URL placement can hide the destination or cover key content.

**How to apply:** Keep a format-aware protected panel with a real QR code and readable fallback destination. For landscape, reserve the side lane outside the media frame; for the stadium card and square layouts, move conflicting lower-third/title copy into the remaining safe area only when a linked export is active.
