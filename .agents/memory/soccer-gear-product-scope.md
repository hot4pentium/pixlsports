**Why:** The user explicitly changed direction: remove jerseys and gear rewards, retire the Soccer Kit editor and Athlete Locker too, keep saved data, and retain the Showcase name with the HYPE Card.

**How to apply:** Remove user-facing routes, navigation, reward affordances, and new reward grants without deleting stored records. Keep the existing Showcase route and HYPE Card sharing behavior; direct athlete involvement toward the card, not another gear destination.
